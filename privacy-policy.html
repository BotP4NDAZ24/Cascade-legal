Privacy Policy

Color Cascade
Last updated: June 2026  ·  Effective immediately

This Privacy Policy explains how Color Cascade ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you play our game. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

We take your privacy seriously. We collect only what is necessary to operate the game and serve advertising, and we never sell your personal data to third parties.

📋

Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Game scores and high scores (stored locally on your device)
  • Daily login streak progress (stored locally on your device)
  • In-game settings preferences (stored locally on your device)

Information collected automatically:

  • Device type, operating system version, and language
  • App usage data such as session length and feature interactions
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA) — only with your explicit consent via the App Tracking Transparency prompt
  • Crash reports and performance data to help us fix bugs

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal identifiers unless you voluntarily contact us for support.

📣

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Color Cascade is free to play and is supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data to serve personalised ads based on your interests.

  • On iOS 14.5+, we will ask for your permission before enabling personalised ads via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework
  • If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be tailored to your interests
  • Google AdMob's data practices are governed by the Google Privacy Policy

You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time in your device Settings → Privacy → Tracking.

🔍

How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the game and save your progress
  • To display relevant advertisements that keep the app free
  • To analyse app performance and fix technical issues
  • To improve the game experience in future updates
  • To respond to support requests you send us

We do not use your data for any purpose beyond operating the App and the advertising services described above.

💾

Data Storage & Retention

All game progress (scores, streaks, settings, coins) is stored locally on your device using browser localStorage / device storage. We do not operate servers that store your game data.

Advertising and analytics data collected by Google AdMob is subject to Google's data retention policies.

You can delete all locally stored game data at any time by uninstalling the App.

👶

Children's Privacy

Color Cascade is rated 4+ on the App Store and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

The advertising SDK (Google AdMob) is configured to serve non-personalised ads to users identified as children, in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable regulations.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child, please contact us immediately at the address below.

⚖️

Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of data we hold about you
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (uninstalling the App deletes local data)
  • Opt-out of personalised advertising: Via device Settings → Privacy → Tracking
  • Withdraw consent: At any time for advertising tracking via the ATT settings

EU/EEA users: You have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). California users: You have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). To exercise any right, contact us at the email below.
